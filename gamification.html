Gamification for Contact Center
UX Case Study

Product Overview

The intent is to enhance agent performance and establish clear career pathways, empowering supervisors to develop tailored career plans for new hires and significantly boost employee engagement. Gamification, through the strategic assignment of targeted games, challenges, awards, enables supervisors to select activities suited to each agent tenure and individual needs, thereby increasing motivation. Furthermore, this approach promotes both social engagement and team building among agent.

Project Duration

31 Aug 2023 - 16 Oct 2023 (6 Weeks)

Responsibility

Researcher

UX/UI Designer along with 2 other designer

Tools

The contact center is facing a challenge in maintaining optimal agent engagement, resulting in decreased interaction and motivation. This requires the development and implementation of effective strategies to improve agent engagement.

Problem Statement

  • Integrate automation into gamification workflows

Automate tasks such as assigning challenges, tracking progress, and distributing rewards, freeing up supervisor time and ensuring consistency.

  • Develop an AI-driven recommendation engine

Create a system that analyzes agent performance data, skill levels, and engagement patterns to provide personalized games, challenges recommendations.

  • Increase agent engagement scores by 15%
  • Automate 80% of routine gamification tasks (e.g., Assign Games, Reward distribution, Agent Performance tracking)
  • Increase agent participation in recommended games by 25% using new gamification

Key Pain Points

  • Lack of recognition for hard work and repetitive tasks
  • Poor communication from supervisors about goals
  • Fear of being micromanaged through gamification
  • Overwhelming call volume during implementation
  • Games becoming repetitive without variety
  • Limited career development pathways

Key Opportunities

  • Position gamification as fun recognition system
  • Clear communication on performance goal connections
  • Frame as support tool, not surveillance
  • Simple games with quick wins and celebrations
  • Rotating game types with personalized rewards
  • Link gamification to skill-building and career growth

Key Takeaways

  • Gamification features like leaderboards and real-time dashboards gave supervisors clearer, faster visibility into agent performance.
  • Supervisors reported feeling more empowered in their daily management routines.
  • Initially, supervisors were overwhelmed by too many metrics; we resolved this with customizable dashboards.
  • Customizable dashboards and simplified metrics helped reduce overwhelm during the onboarding phase.
  • While gamified incentives worked well for some agents, supervisors needed deeper engagement strategies—like team challenges and recognition tools—to sustain participation.
  • Seamless integration with the existing platform made the adoption smoother, minimizing training friction and resistance from supervisors.
  • Close collaboration with product managers helped align gamification features with business KPIs.

Roadmap

  • we would conduct a longitudinal study on user motivation trends.
  • Regular feedback from supervisors helped shape feature priorities, such as the need for drill-down reports and AI generated games, challenges
  • Automatically recommend coaching topics based on gamification performance data.
  • Motivate supervisors with leadership scorecards, coaching challenges, or milestone achievements.
  • Extend gamification models to customer satisfaction (CSAT) and quality monitoring tools.
