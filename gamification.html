Gamification for Contact Center

A gamified engagement system that lets supervisors assign targeted games, challenges and rewards to agents — reducing burnout and lifting performance.

  • Duration6 weeks
  • RoleResearch & UI Design
  • Team3 designers

01 / Overview

Overview

The intent is to enhance agent performance and establish clear career pathways, empowering supervisors to develop tailored career plans for new hires and significantly boost employee engagement.

Gamification, through the strategic assignment of targeted games, challenges and awards, enables supervisors to select activities suited to each agent's tenure and individual needs, increasing motivation. This approach also promotes social engagement and team building among agents.

Project details

Duration: 31 Aug – 16 Oct 2023 (6 weeks)

Responsibility: Researcher & UX/UI Designer, alongside 2 other designers

Tools

  • Figma
  • Google Forms
  • Google Sheets

02 / Problem

Problem & goals

The contact center was facing a challenge in maintaining optimal agent engagement, resulting in decreased interaction and motivation — this called for new strategies to improve agent engagement.

Objectives

  • Integrate automation into gamification workflows. Automate tasks such as assigning challenges, tracking progress and distributing rewards, freeing up supervisor time and ensuring consistency.
  • Develop an AI-driven recommendation engine. Analyze agent performance data, skill levels and engagement patterns to surface personalized game and challenge recommendations.

Goals

  • 15%Increase in agent engagement scores
  • 80%Of routine gamification tasks automated
  • 25%Increase in agent participation in recommended games

Centrical

Strengths

  • Deep integration of learning and development with gamification, creating a holistic approach.
  • Highly personalized gamification experiences driven by AI.
  • Focus on skill development and long-term employee growth through gamified learning.
  • Strong analytics to track the impact of gamification on performance.

Weaknesses

  • Gamification features can be part of a very complex overall system, and therefore sometimes hard to isolate.
  • The sheer number of features can overwhelm users and administrators.
  • May be less focused on quick, simple gamification "wins" and more on long-term strategy.

Genesys

Strengths

  • Gamification as part of a robust workforce engagement management suite.
  • Strong emphasis on performance data and reporting.
  • Scalable for large enterprises with complex needs.
  • Good integration with other contact center tools.

Weaknesses

  • Gamification is not the core focus of the platform.
  • Can be complex to implement and customize.
  • Potentially less emphasis on the "fun" aspects of gamification.

LevelEleven

Strengths

  • Excellent for driving short-term performance improvements through contests and leaderboards.
  • Real-time visibility into performance and progress.
  • Strong focus on sales and service-related KPIs.
  • Very strong at creating a competitive environment.

Weaknesses

  • May not be as effective for long-term engagement or skill development.
  • Can create a high-pressure environment that leads to burnout.
  • Less emphasis on personalized gamification.

Spinify

Strengths

  • Highly visual and engaging gamification experiences.
  • Easy to use and implement, with a focus on quick wins.
  • Strong emphasis on real-time leaderboards and rewards.
  • Good for boosting morale and creating a fun atmosphere.

Weaknesses

  • May lack the depth of features for complex gamification strategies.
  • Potentially less robust analytics and reporting.
  • Less focus on learning and skill development.

amplifAI

Strengths

  • AI-driven coaching and performance insights directly tied to gamification.
  • Strong focus on using gamification to improve specific KPIs.
  • Personalized performance challenges and goals based on individual agent data.
  • Strong on providing managers with data-driven coaching.

Weaknesses

  • Gamification may feel more like performance management than "fun."
  • Less emphasis on visual or social gamification elements.
  • Potentially less game customization.

10 / Outcomes

Key takeaways & roadmap

Key takeaways

  • Gamification features like leaderboards and real-time dashboards gave supervisors clearer, faster visibility into agent performance.
  • Supervisors reported feeling more empowered in their daily management routines.
  • Initially, supervisors were overwhelmed by too many metrics; customizable dashboards resolved this.
  • Simplified metrics helped reduce overwhelm during the onboarding phase.
  • While gamified incentives worked well for some agents, supervisors needed deeper engagement strategies — like team challenges and recognition tools — to sustain participation.
  • Seamless integration with the existing platform made adoption smoother, minimizing training friction and resistance from supervisors.
  • Close collaboration with product managers helped align gamification features with business KPIs.

Roadmap

  • Conduct a longitudinal study on user motivation trends.
  • Use regular supervisor feedback to shape feature priorities, including drill-down reports and AI-generated games and challenges.
  • Automatically recommend coaching topics based on gamification performance data.
  • Motivate supervisors with leadership scorecards, coaching challenges and milestone achievements.
  • Extend gamification models to customer satisfaction (CSAT) and quality monitoring tools.
